02 · The Concept

Sometimes, the first present isn't quite right.

Elle receives an early Christmas present. One sniff, and she walks away. Clearly, it's not what she had in mind.

Later, during a snowy walk in the Swiss Alps, she discovers a Christmas tree glowing among the pines. And beneath it, another present.

This time, things are different.

01
The Wrong Present

At home, Amanda gives Elle an early Christmas gift. Elle sniffs the wrapped present, looks unimpressed and walks away. We never see what's inside.

02
The Winter Walk

Amanda and Elle head out for one of their usual adventures. Elle leads the way through the snow-covered Swiss mountains.

03
An Unexpected Discovery

Elle spots a Christmas tree glowing among the pines.

04
The Second Present

Beneath the tree is another wrapped gift. Amanda opens it.

05
The Perfect Present

Inside is the gift Elle has been waiting for. This time, her reaction is completely different. The final 10 seconds of the Reel show Elle discovering, interacting with and enjoying her new present, with the product clearly visible.

04 · The Partnership

Your Product. Elle's Christmas Wish.

The brand's product is the only gift revealed in the story.

Rather than appearing briefly beneath the Christmas tree, it becomes the focus of the final scene, with Elle discovering and enjoying her present.

The integration is built around her natural behaviour, so the product feels part of her adventure rather than a conventional advertisement.

01
The RevealScene 04

The second present is unwrapped beneath the Christmas tree, revealing the partner's product.

The product and its packaging are clearly visible, giving the brand a recognisable moment within the story.

02
The InteractionScene 05

Elle explores her new gift naturally. Depending on the product, she might play with it, try it, eat it or use it during the adventure.

Filmed in Elle's usual spontaneous style, capturing genuine curiosity and interaction.

03
The Product FocusFinal ~10 sec

Close-ups, Elle interacting with the product and wider shots of her enjoying it ensure the brand is clearly featured without interrupting the storytelling.

The exact sequence is developed with the partner. Elle's comfort and natural behaviour always come first.
